Three things to try

  1. Speed up the rhythm. At the start (rhythm frequency 0.79 Hz, loop latency 60 ms, ±10 ms jitter) the delay costs 360° × 0.79 Hz × 60 ms = 17°, and 83 % of the stimuli land within ±30° of the peak. Slide rhythm frequency to about 10 Hz: the same 60 ms now costs about 216°, as the chapter computes for alpha, within ±30° drops to 0 %, and the stimuli land nearer the trough.
  2. Compensate the mean latency. Still at about 10 Hz, click compensate the mean latency: the mean phase error drops below +10° and about 60 % of the stimuli land within ±30°, but the spread stays near ±34°. A fixed delay can be aimed around; its ±10 ms jitter, ±36° at 10 Hz, cannot.
  3. Bury the rhythm in background EEG. Back at 0.79 Hz with compensation off, set loop latency and latency jitter (±) to 0 ms. At a background EEG of 2 µVrms the spread is ±9° and 100 % land within ±30°; at 60 µVrms the causal filter's zero crossings wander, the spread grows to ±52° and only 59 % land within ±30°.
