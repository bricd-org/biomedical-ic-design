Three things to try

  1. Stretch the interval, fill the packets. At the start (raw ECG, LE 1M, 30 ms, 2 packets) the link fits at 51 % and the radio draws 0.67 mW, half of it on waking up 33 times a second. Slide connection interval to 100 ms (does not fit, 39 kb/s), then raise packets per event to 4: it fits at 85 %, the radio drops to 0.43 mW and 100 mAh lasts 30 days instead of 21, at the price of a 3.25 kB FIFO instead of 0.97 kB.
  2. Turn off data length extension. From the start, switch data length extension off: 20-byte payloads carry only 11 kb/s and raw ECG no longer fits. Even 4 packets per event carry just 21 kb/s, the figure of Worked Example 27.2, and 8 packets 43 kb/s.
  3. Send features, not samples. Choose features (5.2 kb/s needed) at 30 ms: only 4 % used, yet the radio still draws 0.36 mW, nearly all of it on connection events. Slide connection interval to 500 ms: 67 % used, 0.046 mW, and 100 mAh lasts 122 days instead of raw ECG's 21.
