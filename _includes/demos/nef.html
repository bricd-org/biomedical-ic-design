Three things to try

  1. Pay four times for half the noise. From the start (total supply current Itot 2 µA, NEF 3.00) the amplifier makes 0.44 µVrms, the first stage of Worked Example 6.3. Cut Itot to a quarter, 500 nA: the noise only doubles, to 0.88 µVrms, because it falls as 1/√I.
  2. Spend the wet-electrode budget. Keep NEF 3.00 and lower Itot to 177 nA: the noise reaches 1.5 µVrms, the 1.46 µVrms budget the caption says costs 180 nA (Worked Example 6.2). Now drag noise efficiency factor NEF to 1.75, next to the dashed 1.73 MOS-pair limit: at the same current the noise falls in proportion, to 0.86 µVrms.
  3. Widen the band. Go back to 2 µA and NEF 3.00, then click EMG 10–500 Hz. The band is almost five times wider (490 Hz against 99.5 Hz), so the noise rises √5 ≈ 2.2-fold, from 0.44 to 0.97 µVrms: noise power grows in proportion to the band.
