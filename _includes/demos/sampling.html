Three things to try

  1. Fold a tone onto the band edge. At the starting 500 S/s, raise tone frequency to 350 Hz: samples describe reads 150 Hz (aliased), and the dashed 150 Hz wave passes through every sample. This is the fs − fB = 350 Hz tone that lands on the ECG band edge in Worked Example 13.2.
  2. Count 6 dB per bit. Step converter bits N from 8 to 12: SQNR measured climbs from 48.8 to 73.0 dB, tracking 6.02N + 1.76 dB less the 1 dB back-off (48.9 and 73.0 dB).
  3. Back off the tone. At 12 bits, lower tone amplitude from −1 to −20 dBFS: SQNR measured falls from 73.0 to 54.1 dB, one dB for every dB, because the rounding noise stays at LSB2/12 while the signal shrinks. A signal that uses a tenth of the range throws away about three of the twelve bits.
