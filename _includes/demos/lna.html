Three things to try

  1. Rescue the delta band. The demo starts on thin oxide, 1 pair: fL is 4 Hz and the caption warns that the EEG's delta band is lost. Click thick oxide, 27 °C and fL drops to 14.2 mHz, the 0.014 Hz of Worked Example 8.2; then click thick, 85 °C and the same device's corner climbs seventyfold, to 995 mHz.
  2. Trade gain for a lower corner. Back on thin oxide, 1 pair, raise feedback capacitor C2 to its 998 fF maximum. fL falls fivefold to 798 mHz, still above 0.5 Hz, while AM drops to 20 (26.0 dB) and fH rises to 119 kHz, because a lower gain leaves the OTA more loop gain.
  3. Load the output. With C2 back at 200 fF, raise load capacitor CL from 2 pF to 10 pF: fH falls from 23.9 kHz to 4.8 kHz, as in Worked Example 8.1. Push OTA transconductance gm to its 99.8 µS maximum and fH only recovers to 15.8 kHz: bandwidth is bought with OTA current.
