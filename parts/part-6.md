# Wearable Systems
{: .part-title }

A front-end chip is only the start of a wearable. This part follows the signal out of the lab and onto a moving person: how to know when a sample can be trusted and how to clean motion out of it, how to record the brain and the body from the ear, and how to close the loop by acting on what the device measures.
